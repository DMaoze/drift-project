01 / OUR MISSION

Protecting what
we cannot yet see.

Mozambique's deep waters hold extraordinary marine life, but much of it remains poorly documented.

DRIFT is a research initiative focused on understanding the diversity, relative abundance and distribution of deep-water sharks and rays in southern Mozambique. By collecting robust field evidence, we aim to help inform conservation planning and the management of marine protected areas.

How we do our research ↗
02 / OUR RESEARCH

Science at sea.
Evidence for change.

Using non-invasive drifting baited remote underwater video systems (drift BRUVs), we survey offshore habitats around the Bazaruto Archipelago National Park and Vilanculos Coastal Wildlife Sanctuary, including areas beyond protected boundaries.
